# Best Used Cars Under $15,000 in 2026

Finding a reliable used car on a budget requires research. We analyzed hundreds of listings to find the best options.

## Top 3 Picks

### Honda Civic (2016-2018)
Price Range: $12,000-15,000  
Reliability Score: 9.2/10  
These cars regularly reach 200,000 miles with basic maintenance. The 2017 refresh brought better styling.

### Toyota Corolla (2015-2019)
Price Range: $10,000-14,000  
Reliability Score: 9.5/10  
The gold standard. Less exciting than the Civic but even more reliable.

### Mazda3 (2016-2019)
Price Range: $11,000-15,000  
Reliability Score: 8.8/10  
The enthusiast's choice. More fun to drive with comparable reliability.

## Buyer's Checklist
- Service records matter more than mileage
- Always get a pre-purchase inspection
- Check for rust in northern states
- Test drive on highway AND city streets
