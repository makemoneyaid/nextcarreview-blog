# 5 Best Used Cars Under $20,000 That Still Feel New in 2026

Shopping for a used car in 2026 doesn't mean settling for outdated technology or worn-out interiors. With used car prices finally stabilizing after years of inflation, $20,000 now buys you a vehicle with modern safety features, solid fuel economy, and plenty of life left. Here are five standout picks that deliver serious value.

## 1. 2021 Toyota Camry

The Camry remains the gold standard for reliable sedans. The 2021 model introduced Toyota's updated infotainment system with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, plus Toyota Safety Sense 2.5 comes standard. You can find well-maintained examples with under 50,000 miles comfortably within budget.

- **Engine:** 2.5L 4-cylinder (203 hp) or available V6
- **MPG:** Up to 28 city / 39 highway
- **Why it's great:** Legendary reliability, low maintenance costs, strong resale value

## 2. 2019-2020 Honda CR-V

If you need more cargo space, the CR-V is hard to beat. The 2020 refresh brought a hybrid option, but even the standard turbocharged engine delivers a peppy drive. Rear legroom is class-leading, making it a favorite for families.

- **Engine:** 1.5L turbo 4-cylinder (190 hp)
- **MPG:** Up to 28 city / 34 highway
- **Why it's great:** Massive cargo area, comfortable ride, excellent safety ratings

## 3. 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV

Electric vehicles have dropped sharply in price, and the Bolt EV is the best bargain out there. With 259 miles of range and a surprisingly spacious interior for a compact, it's perfect for commuters ready to skip the gas station.

- **Range:** 259 miles (EPA estimated)
- **Charging:** DC fast charging adds 100 miles in 30 minutes
- **Why it's great:** Zero fuel costs, low maintenance, eligible for used EV tax credits

## 4. 2018-2019 Acura RDX

Luxury doesn't have to break the bank. The third-generation RDX (2019+) brought a complete redesign with a twin-scroll turbo engine and Acura's True Touchpad Interface. It drives like a sport sedan but hauls like an SUV.

- **Engine:** 2.0L turbo 4-cylinder (272 hp)
- **MPG:** Up to 22 city / 28 highway
- **Why it's great:** Premium interior, engaging driving dynamics, strong reliability scores

## 5. 2020 Mazda CX-5

Mazda has moved upmarket, and the CX-5 feels more like a luxury vehicle than a mainstream compact SUV. The interior punches well above its price point, and the available turbo engine makes it genuinely fun to drive.

- **Engine:** 2.5L 4-cylinder (187 hp) or available turbo (250 hp)
- **MPG:** Up to 25 city / 31 highway
- **Why it's great:** Upscale cabin, sharp handling, excellent crash test scores

## What to Check Before You Buy

No matter which car you choose, always run a vehicle history report, get a pre-purchase inspection from a trusted mechanic, and compare prices across multiple listings. A little due diligence upfront saves thousands down the road.
